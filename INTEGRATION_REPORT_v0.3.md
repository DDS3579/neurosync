## 4. Known Issues Ranked by Severity

1. **Medium — Unobserved Lighthouse Metrics & Live Offline PWA Reload**: Neither Lighthouse (mobile/desktop) nor a true offline network-cut reload in Chrome DevTools was executed because `lighthouse` is absent and offline SW interception was only audited statically in [public/sw.js](public/sw.js).
2. **Low — Transient `NotFoundError: removeChild` During One `/brain` Panel Reset Transition**: Observed once during the E2E desktop `/brain` reset/drag sequence (and earlier once on `/brain` unmount), without UI crash or recurring across subsequent interactions or reloads. Likely tied to R3F/drei `<Html>` portal unmount timing or toast transition; keep on watch.
3. **Low — Center Label Overlap on Dense Constellations & `Ctrl+wheel` CDP Dispatch Caveat**: On 14-day demo data (`23` stars), two neighboring star labels near the center (`"Box breathing"` / `"Tried something new"`) overlap slightly at `1440x900`. Separately, Playwright's CDP `keyboard.down("Control") + mouse.wheel()` triggered camera zoom rather than brain scale in Chromium (leaving `Size` at `1.00×`), whereas `WheelEvent` with `ctrlKey: true` is verified in [immersiveBrain.test.tsx](src/__tests__/immersiveBrain.test.tsx).
4. **Low — Direct GPU FPS / Heap Evaluation Blocked in Browser Sandbox**: `page.evaluate` is disabled in the browser subagent runner, so `1080p`/`1440p` quality tiers (`data-quality="high"` vs `"low"`, `2400×1350` & `3200×1800` DPR caps, and 20× enter/exit stress cycles) were verified via DOM/canvas bounds rather than raw `requestAnimationFrame` FPS numbers.
